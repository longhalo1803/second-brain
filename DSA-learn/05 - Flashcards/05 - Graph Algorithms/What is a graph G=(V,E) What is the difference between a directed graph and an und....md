### Q: What is a graph $G=(V,E)$? What is the difference between a directed graph and an undirected graph? #card

?
A **graph** is a mathematical data structure defined by the pair $G=(V,E)$, where:

- $V$ is the finite set of **vertices** (or nodes);
- $E \subseteq V \times V$ is the set of **edges**.

Types of graph:

    **Undirected graph:** the pairs in $E$ are *unordered*. The edge $(u,v)$ is identical to $(v,u)$.




    **Directed graph:** the pairs in $E$ are *ordered*. The edge $(u,v)$ goes from node $u$ to node $v$ and is different from edge $(v,u)$.
