### Q: What are the characteristic dimensions of a graph and how is computational cost expressed? #card

?
The dimensions of a graph $G=(V,E)$ are defined by two main parameters:

$n = |V|$: number of vertices (nodes)
$m = |E|$: number of edges

The computational cost (time and space complexity) of graph algorithms is usually expressed as a function of both $n$ and $m$ (for example $\Theta(n+m)$, $O(m \log n)$ or $O(n^2)$).
