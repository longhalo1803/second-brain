# 🎴 What is a binary tree and why are its children not interchangeable

---

### Q: What is a binary tree and why are its children not interchangeable? #card

?
A **binary tree** is a rooted tree in which each node has **zero, one, or two children**, identified specifically as the **left child** and the **right child**.

⚠️ Warning:
The left child and the right child are strictly defined by the **tree structure**. Two trees that differ only in the position of a child (for example, a single child placed on the left instead of the right) **are not equivalent** and cannot be interchanged.
