### Q: [REFERENCE] In well-known algorithms such as Dijkstra, Prim, Huffman, how is the Priority Queue used, in brief (what has highest priority)? #card

?
The priority queue is the backbone data structure for several fundamental graph and coding algorithms:

AlgorithmPair in Queue `(el, pr)`Highest priority**Dijkstra** (shortest paths)`(node, distance from source)`Node with minimum distance**Prim** (minimum spanning tree)`(node/edge, weight of edge)`Node with minimum edge weight**Huffman** (variable-length codes)`(character set, sum of frequencies)`Set with minimum frequency
