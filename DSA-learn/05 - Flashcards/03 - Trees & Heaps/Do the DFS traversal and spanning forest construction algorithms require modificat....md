### Q: Do the DFS traversal and spanning forest construction algorithms require modifications when applied to a directed graph? #card

?
**No.** The DFS traversal and spanning forest calculation algorithms work **without any modification** also in the case of directed graphs.

📌 Note:
The only difference compared to undirected graphs lies in the greater variety of edge types that can be encountered (forward and cross edges, in addition to back edges).
