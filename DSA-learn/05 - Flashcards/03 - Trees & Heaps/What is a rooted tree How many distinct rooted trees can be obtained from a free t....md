### Q: What is a rooted tree? How many distinct rooted trees can be obtained from a free tree with $n$ nodes? #card

?
A **rooted tree** is a free tree in which one node is chosen as the **root** and the edges are considered directed from parents to children.

📌 Note:
From a single free tree composed of $n$ nodes, it is possible to obtain $n$** distinct rooted trees**, depending on which node is chosen as the root.
