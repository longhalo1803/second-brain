# 🎴 What is the expected computational cost of Randomized-QuickSort

---

### Q: What is the expected computational cost of Randomized-QuickSort? #card

?
**Theorem:** the expected number of comparisons between array elements performed by the `QuickSort` algorithm with a randomly chosen pivot is:

$$
\Theta(n \log n)
$$

Therefore, the average/expected case complexity of Randomized-QuickSort is equal to $\Theta(n \log n)$.
