### Q: What does $T(n)$ denote? #card

?
$T(n)$ is the **computational cost** of an algorithm, namely the maximum number of elementary operations executed by the code snippet, as a function of $n$.

📌 Note: _elementary operations_ generally refer to **assignments **and **logical-arithmetic operations**.
