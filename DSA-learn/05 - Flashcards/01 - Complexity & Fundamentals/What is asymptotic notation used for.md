# 🎴 What is asymptotic notation used for

### Q: What is asymptotic notation used for? #card

?
It is used to estimate the upper and lower bounds of the computational cost of algorithms and problems, namely:

- estimate the maximum execution time,
- understand the growth of execution time as the
  size $n$ of input instances grows,
- compare the performance of different algorithms for the same problem,
- establish the difficulty of problems.
