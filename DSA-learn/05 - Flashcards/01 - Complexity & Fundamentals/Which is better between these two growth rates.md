### Q: Which is better between these two growth rates:

$\Omega(1)$ or $\Omega(n^2)$? #card
?
$\Omega(1)$ is better from an algorithmic complexity standpoint.
In fact, $\Omega$ indicates the lower bound, meaning that an algorithm performs *at least *as many operations as that order, so the lower it is, the better.If it is constant, meaning it does not increase as $n$ increases, it is the best one can do.
