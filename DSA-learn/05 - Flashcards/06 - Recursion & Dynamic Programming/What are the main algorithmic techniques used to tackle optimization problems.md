### Q: What are the main algorithmic techniques used to tackle optimization problems? #card

?
Two important techniques for solving optimization problems are:

- **GREEDY Algorithms:** incrementally build the solution, making at each step a choice that optimizes the cost of the partial solution constructed up to that point.
- **DYNAMIC PROGRAMMING Algorithms:** solve complex problems by combining the solutions of related subproblems (tackled successively).
  📌 Conceptual difference:
  The Greedy approach makes immediate and irrevocable local decisions at each iteration, whereas Dynamic Programming examines multiple options while storing sub-results.
