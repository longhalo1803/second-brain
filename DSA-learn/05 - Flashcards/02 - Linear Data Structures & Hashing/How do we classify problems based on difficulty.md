### Q: How do we classify problems based on difficulty? #card

?

- **Tractable**: have an algorithm with polynomial computational cost (i.e. $T(n) \in O(n^k)$).
- **Presumably intractable**: no known polynomial-time algorithm (but not proven that none exists).
- **Intractable**: *proven *that no polynomial-cost algorithm exists to solve them.
- **Unsolvable**: *proven *that no algorithm exists (regardless of cost).
