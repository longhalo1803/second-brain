### Q: 📝 2. Let $f(n)=n+2n^3-3n^3+4n^4$.

State which of the following statements are true and which are false. Justify your answers.
(a) $f(n) \in \Omega(n \log n)$
(b) $f(n) \in \Theta(n^5)$
(c) $f(n) \in O(n^{10})$
(d) $f(n) \in \Omega(n^4)$ #card
?
We have $f(n)=n+2n^3-3n^3+4n^4=4n^4-n^3+n \in \Theta(n^4)$.
Therefore, based on the tables:
(a) True;
(b) False;
(c) True;
(d) True.
